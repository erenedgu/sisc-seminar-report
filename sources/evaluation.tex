\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experimental-setup}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Testing the Necessity of UQ in Our Specific Case
      \begin{itemize}
          \item Five different ensembles with noise levels $\sigma \in \{0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1.0\}$ meters were created.
          \item Each ensemble consisted of 100 equiprobable realizations.
          \item The Quantity of Interest (QoI) was the water depth at [925.0, 605.0].
          \item The mean, standard deviation, and coefficient of variation (CV) were calculated for each ensemble.
      \end{itemize}
  \item Testing the validity of using time-to-solution as a proxy for computational energy costs.
      \begin{itemize}
          \item Three different versions of the original DEM were created with different resolutions. Cell sizes were namely 10.0~m, 12.0~m, 15.0~m, and 20.0~m. (Side by side figure?)
          \item For each version, ensembles with 100 iterations were created with a zero noise level of $\sigma = 0.0$ meters.
          \item The mean, standard deviation, and coefficient of variation (CV) were calculated for each ensemble.
      \end{itemize}
  \item Uncertainty Propagation of Energy Consumption
      \begin{itemize}
          \item The same ensembles ($\sigma \in \{0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1.0\}$ meters) from the first experiment were used to assess the effects of uncertainty propagation in energy consumption.
          \item The mean, standard deviation, and coefficient of variation (CV) were calculated for each ensemble.
      \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
